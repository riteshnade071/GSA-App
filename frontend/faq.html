Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about OpportunityHub.

What is OpportunityHub?

A free website that gathers government scholarships, farmer schemes and career guidance for India and shows the ones that match your details.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no fee to create an account, see matches or use any section.

Is this a government website?

No. We are an independent project. We link to the official source of every scheme so you can apply there.

Do you apply for me?

No. We show you what you may be eligible for and the steps to follow. You apply yourself on the official portal.

How do I sign up?

Tap "Continue with Google", or enter your name, mobile number and email and verify it with the 6-digit code we send. Your mobile number is your login ID.

I did not get the verification code.

Check your Spam or Promotions folder, wait for the Resend button, and make sure the email is typed correctly. Codes are valid for 5 minutes. If it still does not arrive, try "Continue with Google" or contact us.

I forgot my password.

On the login screen tap "Forgot password?", enter your mobile number and the email on your account, and use the code we send to set a new one. Accounts created with Google log in with the Google button.

How are scholarships matched to me?

We compare the details you enter (state, education, marks, income, category and so on) with each scheme's published rules. Fields you leave empty never block a match; we simply mark them as "check".

Is the information always correct?

We try hard, but deadlines and rules can change. Always confirm on the official website before applying.

Is my data safe? Do you sell it?

We never sell your data. Passwords are stored hashed. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

Email us at zalteom111@gmail.com from your registered email or mention your mobile number, and we will delete it.

The site is slow the first time I open it.

Our server sleeps when nobody has used it for a while. The first request can take 30 to 50 seconds; after that it is fast.
