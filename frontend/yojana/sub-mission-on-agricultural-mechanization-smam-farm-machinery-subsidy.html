Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM) — farm machinery subsidy

Central scheme that helps farmers buy tractors, power tillers, harvesters, sprayers and other implements, and helps set up custom hiring centres. The states manage the applications.

Amount / benefit: Typically 40% of machine cost, 50% for SC/ST, women and small/marginal farmers, with per-machine caps

Source: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India

What you get

Subsidy paid into the farmer's bank account after purchase and verification. Rates and caps depend on the machine and your category, so the list on your state portal is the one that counts.

Who is eligible

Farmers buying approved machinery, with priority for SC/ST, women, and small and marginal farmers. Most states allow a subsidy on the same machine type only once in several years. Machines must be on your state's approved list.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Land record
  • Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
  • Caste certificate (SC/ST applicants)
  • Quotation or invoice for the machine

How to apply, step by step

  1. Apply on your state's farmer-scheme portal (in Maharashtra: MahaDBT, Farmer Schemes, Agricultural Mechanization).
  2. Choose the machine from the approved list and upload your documents.
  3. If selected, buy the machine from an approved dealer and upload the invoice.
  4. After verification the subsidy is credited to your bank account.

Last date

Most farmer schemes accept applications through the year or in notified windows. Check the official portal for the current window.

Official website

https://agrimachinery.nic.in (Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India)
