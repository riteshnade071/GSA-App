Bhavantar Bharpai Yojana — Haryana price-difference compensation

Haryana Horticulture Department scheme that protects vegetable and fruit growers from low market prices. If a registered farmer sells below the protected price within the notified period, the government pays the difference. The scheme has also been extended to potato and, with a per-acre relief, to bajra. You must register the crop on Meri Fasal Mera Byora during the sowing period, sell through a J-Form at the market committee, and the sales details are uploaded on the BBY e-portal.

Amount / benefit: Varies by crop

Source: Department of Horticulture, Government of Haryana

What you get

The gap between the protected price and your selling price (or the per-acre relief for notified crops), paid to your Aadhaar-linked bank account.

Who is eligible

Landowners, lessees and tenant farmers in Haryana who registered the notified crop on Meri Fasal Mera Byora during the sowing period and sold it through the market committee with a J-Form.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Registration proof from Meri Fasal Mera Byora
  • Land ownership proof, or lease agreement for tenants
  • J-Form of the sale from the market committee
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account

How to apply, step by step

  1. Register your crop on Meri Fasal Mera Byora during the sowing period (free).
  2. Wait for the Horticulture Department to verify your crop.
  3. Sell your produce at the market through a J-Form. Each market committee office can upload the sale details on the BBY e-portal.
  4. Check your claim status on the portal and make sure your bank account is Aadhaar-linked, because the money is paid by DBT.

Last date

Most farmer schemes accept applications through the year or in notified windows. Check the official portal for the current window.
