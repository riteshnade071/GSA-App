Indiramma Atmiya Bharosa (Telangana, landless agricultural families)

Telangana's support for landless farm-labour families, announced together with Rythu Bharosa. Families are identified from MGNREGS job-card records.

Amount / benefit: ₹12,000 a year per eligible landless agricultural family

Source: Government of Telangana

What you get

₹12,000 a year paid by direct bank transfer to eligible landless agricultural families.

Who is eligible

Landless agricultural families of Telangana in which a member worked at least 20 days under MGNREGS in 2023-24, as stated in the January 2025 guidelines. The state may have updated the criteria since, so confirm the current rules with your Mandal office.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • MGNREGS job card
  • Aadhaar card
  • Bank account details linked with Aadhaar
  • Ration card (if the office asks for it)

How to apply, step by step

  1. Check that your MGNREGS job card and work days are correctly recorded.
  2. Ask your Mandal or Gram Panchayat office whether your family is on the eligible list.
  3. Keep your Aadhaar-linked bank account details up to date for the transfer.

Last date

Most farmer schemes accept applications through the year or in notified windows. Check the official portal for the current window.
