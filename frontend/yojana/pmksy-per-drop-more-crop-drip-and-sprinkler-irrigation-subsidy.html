PMKSY – Per Drop More Crop (drip and sprinkler irrigation subsidy)

Central support for installing drip and sprinkler (micro-irrigation) systems to save water and raise yield. The states run it through their agriculture or horticulture departments.

Amount / benefit: 55% subsidy for small and marginal farmers, 45% for other farmers, up to 5 hectares

Source: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India

What you get

Financial assistance of 55% of the cost for small and marginal farmers and 45% for others, with the subsidy limited to 5 hectares per beneficiary. Some states add a top-up.

Who is eligible

Farmers who install a drip or sprinkler system on their land. Small and marginal farmers get the higher rate. The subsidy ceiling is 5 hectares per beneficiary. Your state decides the application window and selection method.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Land record
  • Bank passbook
  • Caste certificate (only if you claim a category benefit)
  • Quotation or bill from an approved supplier, as your state requires

How to apply, step by step

  1. Apply on your state's farmer-scheme portal (in Maharashtra this is MahaDBT) and choose the micro-irrigation scheme.
  2. Upload your land record, Aadhaar and bank details.
  3. After approval, buy and install the system from an approved supplier.
  4. Officials inspect the installation and the subsidy is paid to your bank account.

Last date

Most farmer schemes accept applications through the year or in notified windows. Check the official portal for the current window.

Official website

https://pmksy.gov.in (Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India)
