Annadata Sukhibhava – PM-KISAN (Andhra Pradesh)

Andhra Pradesh's farmer support scheme, launched on 2 August 2025 and paid together with PM-KISAN. The first instalment of each year is ₹7,000 (₹5,000 from the state and ₹2,000 from PM-KISAN).

Amount / benefit: ₹20,000 a year: ₹6,000 from PM-KISAN plus ₹14,000 from the state, in three instalments

Source: Agriculture Department, Government of Andhra Pradesh

What you get

₹14,000 a year from the state on top of the ₹6,000 PM-KISAN money, by direct bank transfer in three instalments.

Who is eligible

Eligible farmer families of Andhra Pradesh who are current PM-KISAN beneficiaries, with only one farmer per family paid. The state's information also mentions small, marginal and tenant farmers. Because the scheme uses the PM-KISAN database, a PM-KISAN problem (such as pending eKYC) can block this payment too.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • PM-KISAN registration number
  • Aadhaar card
  • Bank account linked with Aadhaar
  • Land record (tenant farmers: tenancy / cultivator card as the department asks)

How to apply, step by step

  1. Check that you are registered on PM-KISAN and that your eKYC is complete.
  2. Check your name and status on the Annadata Sukhibhava portal.
  3. Tenant farmers and anyone left out should contact the district or mandal Agriculture Department office.
  4. Keep your Aadhaar-linked bank account active so the instalments reach you.

Last date

Most farmer schemes accept applications through the year or in notified windows. Check the official portal for the current window.

Official website

https://annadathasukhibhava.ap.gov.in (Agriculture Department, Government of Andhra Pradesh)
