Kisan Credit Card (KCC) with interest subvention

Revolving short-term credit for seeds, fertiliser and other crop needs, with government interest support. Also available for animal husbandry and fisheries. Budget 2025-26 announced a rise in the loan limit from ₹3 lakh to ₹5 lakh, so ask your bank for the limit that applies today.

Amount / benefit: Crop loans at 7% interest, effectively 4% with timely repayment (interest-subvention scheme, loans up to ₹3 lakh)

Source: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / RBI / NABARD

What you get

Short-term crop loans at 7% a year; an extra 3% prompt-repayment incentive brings it to 4%. Loans up to ₹2 lakh per borrower can be taken without collateral.

Who is eligible

Farmers who cultivate land, including owner-cultivators, tenant farmers, oral lessees and sharecroppers, and groups of farmers (self-help or joint liability groups). Fishers and animal-husbandry farmers can also apply. The bank sets your limit from your land, crops and needs. To get the 4% rate you must repay on time.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Bank's KCC application form
  • Aadhaar / identity proof
  • Address proof
  • Land record or tenancy proof
  • Passport-size photographs

How to apply, step by step

  1. Go to your bank branch, rural bank or primary agricultural cooperative society and ask for the Kisan Credit Card form. If you are a PM-KISAN beneficiary, your PM-KISAN details can simplify the form.
  2. Fill the form and attach your identity, address and land documents.
  3. The bank checks your land and crop details and fixes your limit.
  4. Use the card within the limit and repay on time to keep the interest benefit.

Last date

Most farmer schemes accept applications through the year or in notified windows. Check the official portal for the current window.

Official website

https://www.nabard.org (Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / RBI / NABARD)
