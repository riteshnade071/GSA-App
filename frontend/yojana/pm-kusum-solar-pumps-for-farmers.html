PM-KUSUM — solar pumps for farmers

Central scheme (MNRE) to replace diesel pumps with solar pumps and to solarise existing grid-connected pumps. Component B is standalone solar pumps; Component C is solarising existing grid pumps. The states run the applications.

Amount / benefit: Up to 60% subsidy on a standalone solar pump up to 7.5 HP (30% Centre + 30% State); you pay 40%, of which a bank loan can cover 30%

Source: Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), Government of India

What you get

Central subsidy of 30% of the benchmark cost (50% in the North-East and hill states) plus at least 30% from the state. With a bank loan for 30%, the farmer's upfront share can be as low as 10%.

Who is eligible

Farmers who need an irrigation solar pump up to 7.5 HP, bought from an approved vendor. The states notify who can apply and in what order, so check your state's portal. Subsidy is calculated on the MNRE benchmark cost, not your invoice.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Land ownership or lease record
  • Bank account details
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Electricity connection details (for solarising an existing grid pump)

How to apply, step by step

  1. Open pmkusum.mnre.gov.in and follow the link to your state's nodal agency.
  2. Register on the state portal with your Aadhaar and mobile number.
  3. Choose pump capacity and an approved vendor, and upload your documents.
  4. When the demand letter arrives, pay your share (a bank loan can cover part of it).
  5. The vendor installs the pump and the state agency inspects it.

Last date

Most farmer schemes accept applications through the year or in notified windows. Check the official portal for the current window.
