PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

Central income-support scheme for farmer families that own cultivable land. Money goes straight into the farmer's bank account.

Amount / benefit: ₹6,000 a year, paid in 3 instalments of ₹2,000

Source: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India

What you get

₹6,000 per family per year in three equal instalments of ₹2,000 every four months, by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

Who is eligible

A farmer family that owns cultivable land recorded in the state land records, with an Aadhaar-linked bank account. eKYC is mandatory. Not eligible: institutional landholders, current or former holders of constitutional posts, income-tax payers, professionals such as doctors, engineers and lawyers, and retired pensioners getting ₹10,000 or more a month (multi-tasking/Group D staff excepted). Only one member of a family is paid. Land bought after 1 Feb 2019 can be held for physical verification.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Bank account linked with Aadhaar
  • Land ownership record of your state
  • Mobile number linked to Aadhaar (for OTP eKYC)

How to apply, step by step

  1. Open pmkisan.gov.in, go to Farmers Corner and choose New Farmer Registration (or visit your nearest CSC).
  2. Choose rural or urban farmer, enter your Aadhaar number and state, and let the portal check whether you are already registered.
  3. Fill in your personal, bank and land details exactly as they appear in your records.
  4. Complete eKYC: OTP-based eKYC on the portal, or biometric eKYC at a Common Service Centre.
  5. State officials verify your land and details. After approval the instalments arrive by DBT; use 'Know Your Status' on the portal to follow it.

Last date

Most farmer schemes accept applications through the year or in notified windows. Check the official portal for the current window.
