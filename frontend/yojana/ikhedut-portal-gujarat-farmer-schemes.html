iKhedut Portal (Gujarat farmer schemes)

Gujarat's online portal where farmers apply for agriculture, horticulture, animal husbandry and fisheries schemes of the state government, including subsidies on farm equipment and inputs. Each scheme has its own conditions and its own application window.

Amount / benefit: Varies by scheme

Source: Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare & Cooperation, Government of Gujarat

What you get

Subsidy or assistance under the scheme you choose, paid to your bank account after verification.

Who is eligible

Farmers who live in Gujarat and have valid documents. Some schemes have extra conditions, for example some are for SC/ST farmers only or exclude them, and items usually must be bought from an authorised dealer.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Land records
  • Bank passbook
  • Caste certificate (for SC/ST schemes)
  • Other papers listed under the scheme you choose

How to apply, step by step

  1. Open ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in and look through the scheme list.
  2. Choose the scheme and fill in the online form.
  3. Upload your documents and confirm the application.
  4. Save or print the application and note the application number. You can check the status in the Applicant Corner.

Last date

Most farmer schemes accept applications through the year or in notified windows. Check the official portal for the current window.

Official website

https://ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in (Department of Agriculture, Farmers Welfare & Cooperation, Government of Gujarat)
