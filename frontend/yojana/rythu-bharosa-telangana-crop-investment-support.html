Rythu Bharosa (Telangana crop investment support)

Telangana's crop investment support. It replaced Rythu Bandhu from 26 January 2025 and is paid straight to the farmer's bank account. For Kharif 2026 the state began releasing ₹6,000 per acre from 30 June 2026.

Amount / benefit: ₹12,000 per acre a year, paid as ₹6,000 per acre in each of the two seasons (Kharif and Rabi)

Source: Agriculture & Cooperation Department, Government of Telangana

What you get

₹6,000 per acre per season by direct bank transfer for land the state treats as cultivable. Farmers holding forest podu land under the Recognition of Forest Rights Act are also covered.

Who is eligible

Farmers of Telangana with agricultural land that is fit for cultivation. Not eligible: uncultivated land, hills with mining, land taken for roads, farm land converted for real estate or industry, and land acquired by the state. Land is checked through joint surveys by agriculture and revenue officials using survey numbers on the Bhu Bharati portal, and by village meetings (gram sabhas). Rules and amounts are set by the state, so check the current guidelines before relying on this.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Land records / pattadar passbook (survey numbers on the Bhu Bharati portal)
  • Bank account details linked with Aadhaar
  • Aadhaar card
  • Mobile number

How to apply, step by step

  1. Make sure your land and survey number are correctly recorded on the Bhu Bharati land portal.
  2. Attend or check the village gram sabha lists, where eligible land is announced. Contact your Mandal Agriculture Officer if your land was left out.
  3. Keep your bank account details up to date so the money can be sent by DBT.
  4. Check your payment status on the Rythu Bharosa portal. If it says processed but you got no money, ask the Mandal Agriculture Office.

Last date

Most farmer schemes accept applications through the year or in notified windows. Check the official portal for the current window.
