Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF) — loans for storage, processing and farm-gate projects

A long-term loan facility for post-harvest and community farming projects such as cold storage, warehouses, sorting and grading, and processing units. Open to farmers, FPOs, cooperatives, self-help groups and agri-entrepreneurs.

Amount / benefit: 3% a year interest subvention on loans up to ₹2 crore, for up to 7 years

Source: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India

What you get

3% interest subvention on loans up to ₹2 crore for a maximum of 7 years, and credit-guarantee cover (CGTMSE) for loans up to ₹2 crore, so no collateral is needed up to that limit. One applicant can set up to 25 projects in different locations.

Who is eligible

Farmers, FPOs, primary agricultural credit societies, marketing cooperatives, self-help groups, joint liability groups, agri-entrepreneurs, start-ups and public-private projects with a viable post-harvest or farm-gate project. The scheme runs till 2032-33.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Identity and address proof
  • Project report
  • Land or lease documents for the project site
  • Bank statements
  • Registration papers for FPO / society / firm, if applicable

How to apply, step by step

  1. Prepare a project report for your storage, processing or farm-gate project.
  2. Apply on the AIF portal (agriinfra.dac.gov.in) or at a participating bank.
  3. The bank appraises the project and sanctions the loan with the interest subvention.
  4. Build the project and keep your repayments on schedule.

Last date
