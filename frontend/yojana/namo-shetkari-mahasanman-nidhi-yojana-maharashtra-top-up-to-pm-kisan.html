Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi Yojana (Maharashtra top-up to PM-KISAN)

Maharashtra government's additional income support for farmer families who already receive PM-KISAN. Launched in October 2023.

Amount / benefit: ₹6,000 a year in 3 instalments (a rise to ₹9,000 was reported in Feb 2025 — confirm the current amount on the official portal)

Source: Agriculture Department, Government of Maharashtra

What you get

Extra money every year, paid in three instalments by DBT on top of the ₹6,000 from PM-KISAN.

Who is eligible

Farmer families of Maharashtra who are PM-KISAN beneficiaries, with cultivable land in their name and an Aadhaar-linked bank account. You must first be registered on the PM-KISAN portal.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • PM-KISAN registration
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • Registered mobile number
  • Land record

How to apply, step by step

  1. Register on the PM-KISAN portal (pmkisan.gov.in) and finish eKYC.
  2. Make sure your Aadhaar-linked bank account and land details are correct.
  3. Check your payment status on the Namo Shetkari portal.

Last date

Most farmer schemes accept applications through the year or in notified windows. Check the official portal for the current window.

Official website

https://nsmny.mahait.org (Agriculture Department, Government of Maharashtra)
