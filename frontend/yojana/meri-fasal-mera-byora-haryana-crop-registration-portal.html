Meri Fasal Mera Byora — Haryana crop registration portal

Haryana's 'My Crop, My Record' portal. Farmers register their land and the crops they sow during the sowing season. Registration is needed for Bhavantar Bharpai Yojana compensation, and it is also used for MSP procurement and crop insurance. Late registration can cost you these benefits, and false entries are removed by the district administration.

What you get

No money by itself. Registration lets you claim MSP procurement, Bhavantar Bharpai compensation and crop-insurance cover.

Who is eligible

Farmers of Haryana: landowners, lessees and tenant farmers (tenants need a lease agreement). Registration must be done for the crops you actually sow, within the sowing period.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) details
  • Aadhaar-linked mobile number
  • Proof of land ownership, or lease agreement for tenant farmers
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account

How to apply, step by step

  1. Open the Meri Fasal Mera Byora portal and log in with your PPP details and mobile OTP.
  2. Select your land and enter the crop you have sown, and the area, for the current season.
  3. Submit. The Agriculture or Horticulture Department checks (and may physically verify) your entry.
  4. Check on the portal that your crop shows as verified before the registration window closes.

Last date

Most farmer schemes accept applications through the year or in notified windows. Check the official portal for the current window.
