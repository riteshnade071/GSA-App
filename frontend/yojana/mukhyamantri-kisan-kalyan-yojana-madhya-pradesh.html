Mukhyamantri Kisan Kalyan Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

Madhya Pradesh's top-up for farmers who already get PM-KISAN. It began in September 2020 and the state government has said it will continue.

Amount / benefit: ₹6,000 a year in three instalments of ₹2,000, on top of PM-KISAN (₹12,000 in total)

Source: Farmer Welfare & Agriculture Development Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh

What you get

An extra ₹6,000 a year, paid in three instalments of ₹2,000 by direct bank transfer, in addition to the ₹6,000 from PM-KISAN.

Who is eligible

Permanent residents of Madhya Pradesh who are registered under PM-KISAN with eKYC done and who have arable land they farm. Income-tax payers, elected representatives and government employees are not eligible.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • PM-KISAN registration with eKYC
  • Aadhaar card
  • Bank account linked with Aadhaar
  • Land record

How to apply, step by step

  1. Register on PM-KISAN (pmkisan.gov.in) and finish eKYC.
  2. Check that your land and bank details are correct in the records.
  3. Contact your district or block agriculture office if the state instalment does not arrive.

Last date

Most farmer schemes accept applications through the year or in notified windows. Check the official portal for the current window.
