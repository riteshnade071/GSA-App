MahaDBT Farmer Schemes (Maharashtra online portal)

Maharashtra's single online portal for farmer schemes such as agricultural mechanization (including tractors and other farm machinery), micro-irrigation and other agriculture department subsidies. You register once and apply to individual schemes from your login.

Amount / benefit: Varies by scheme: machinery subsidy is typically 40–50%; micro-irrigation 45–55%

Source: Government of Maharashtra (Agriculture Department)

What you get

Subsidy is paid directly to the farmer's bank account after the purchase or installation is verified.

Who is eligible

Farmers resident in Maharashtra with land records and an Aadhaar-linked bank account. Each scheme on the portal has its own conditions, and SC/ST, women and small/marginal farmers get priority or a higher rate in many of them.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card (linked to bank account)
  • Land records (7/12 and 8-A extracts)
  • Bank passbook
  • Caste certificate (SC/ST applicants)
  • Quotation or invoice for the item, as the scheme requires

How to apply, step by step

  1. Go to mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in and register or log in as a farmer.
  2. Complete your profile with Aadhaar, land and bank details.
  3. Open the Farmer Schemes section and choose a scheme, for example Agricultural Mechanization.
  4. Fill in the form, upload documents and submit.
  5. Track your application from your login and follow the instructions if you are selected.

Last date

Most farmer schemes accept applications through the year or in notified windows. Check the official portal for the current window.

Official website

https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in (Government of Maharashtra (Agriculture Department))
