Crop insurance in Maharashtra (PMFBY farmer share from 2025)

Maharashtra stopped the arrangement in which the state paid the whole farmer share of the PMFBY premium in 2025, according to a Government of India release of August 2026. Farmers therefore pay the normal PMFBY share. Enrolment in Kharif 2025 fell sharply because of this.

Amount / benefit: Standard PMFBY share: 2% (Kharif), 1.5% (Rabi food crops and oilseeds), 5% (commercial/horticulture)

Source: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India

What you get

Same cover as PMFBY: notified crops in notified areas, with claims paid to the farmer's bank account.

Who is eligible

Farmers growing notified crops in notified areas of Maharashtra, including tenant farmers and sharecroppers. The state sets the last date for each crop and season.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Bank account details / passbook
  • Land record (7/12) or tenancy / sharecropping agreement
  • Sowing declaration
  • Mobile number

How to apply, step by step

  1. Check the last date for your crop and district on pmfby.gov.in or at your taluka agriculture office.
  2. Enrol through your bank, a CSC, or yourself on pmfby.gov.in.
  3. Pay your share of the premium and keep the receipt.
  4. Report crop loss within 72 hours of a localised calamity.

Last date

Most farmer schemes accept applications through the year or in notified windows. Check the official portal for the current window.

Official website

https://pmfby.gov.in (Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India)
