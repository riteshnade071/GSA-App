Mukhyamantri Saur Krushi Pump Yojana (PM-KUSUM in Maharashtra)

Maharashtra's way of running PM-KUSUM solar pumps for farmers, with pump sizes of 3, 5 and 7.5 HP. Applications go through the state's power distribution company.

Amount / benefit: Large subsidy on 3, 5 and 7.5 HP solar pumps; farmer's share reported as about 10% — confirm with MSEDCL

Source: MSEDCL / Government of Maharashtra under MNRE's PM-KUSUM

What you get

Central and state subsidy covers most of the pump cost; the farmer pays a small share. Exact shares and category-wise amounts are on the state notification.

Who is eligible

Maharashtra farmers who need a solar irrigation pump and meet the state's conditions. Some areas, for example where groundwater is restricted, can be excluded, so check your village with your MSEDCL or taluka agriculture office.

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Land record (7/12 extract)
  • Bank account details
  • Water source details (well or borewell)
  • Caste certificate (if claiming a category rate)

How to apply, step by step

  1. Open pmkusum.mnre.gov.in and follow the Maharashtra link, or ask your MSEDCL or taluka agriculture office where applications are open.
  2. Register and fill in the application with your land and water-source details.
  3. Pay your share when you receive the demand note.
  4. The vendor installs the pump and it is inspected.

Last date

Most farmer schemes accept applications through the year or in notified windows. Check the official portal for the current window.
