Telangana ePASS Post-Matric Scholarship — BC / Minority

For BC and Minority students who are permanent residents of Telangana in a recognised Telangana institution. Family income up to ₹2 lakh in urban areas and ₹1.5 lakh in rural areas (the rural limit is not checked here). Full fee reimbursement applies only in the cases covered by the government orders (for example government junior college or specified EAMCET/ECET ranks). The document list below may be shorter than the portal's.

Amount / benefit: 100% tuition fee + maintenance allowance

Source: Telangana ePASS (SC / ST / BC / Minority Welfare Departments)

What you get

Full tuition fee reimbursement (paid to the college) plus a monthly maintenance allowance to your bank account (per scheme summaries — confirm on ePASS).

Who is eligible

  • Linked to Telangana (home state or institute state)
  • Category: OBC, Minority community
  • Family income up to ₹2 lakh per year

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • BC / minority certificate (MeeSeva)
  • Income certificate (MeeSeva / MRO, dated on or after 1 Apr 2026)
  • Aadhaar card (name must match SSC certificate)
  • SSC / previous marksheet
  • Bank passbook

How to apply, step by step

  1. Open the Telangana ePASS portal (telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in) and start a fresh registration.
  2. Get your caste certificate and income certificate from a MeeSeva centre first. For 2026-27 the income certificate must be dated on or after 1 April 2026, and the name on your Aadhaar must exactly match your SSC certificate.
  3. Fill in the form, upload the documents on this card and submit it.
  4. Your college and the department verify the application. The tuition fee goes to the college and the maintenance allowance to your Aadhaar-seeded bank account. Track the status on the portal.
हिंदी में आवेदन कैसे करें
  1. तेलंगाना ePASS पोर्टल (telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in) खोलें और नया (fresh) रजिस्ट्रेशन शुरू करें।
  2. पहले MeeSeva केंद्र से जाति प्रमाण पत्र और आय प्रमाण पत्र बनवाएं। 2026-27 के लिए आय प्रमाण पत्र 1 अप्रैल 2026 या उसके बाद की तारीख का होना चाहिए, और आधार पर नाम आपके SSC प्रमाण पत्र से बिल्कुल मेल खाना चाहिए।
  3. फॉर्म भरें, इस कार्ड के दस्तावेज़ अपलोड करें और सबमिट करें।
  4. आपका कॉलेज और विभाग आवेदन का सत्यापन करते हैं। ट्यूशन फीस कॉलेज को और मेंटेनेंस भत्ता आपके आधार-सीडेड बैंक खाते में जाता है। पोर्टल पर स्थिति देखते रहें।
