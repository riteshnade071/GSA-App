Mukhyamantri Yuva Swavalamban Yojana (MYSY) — Gujarat

Merit-cum-means support for students of all categories who are Gujarat residents and have taken admission in a Gujarat diploma or degree course. Family income up to ₹6 lakh. Fresh applicants need 80 percentile (not percentage) in Class 12 Science or General stream for degree courses, or in Class 10 for diploma. The deadline was extended; one report gives 30 Oct 2026, 6 PM, with document verification by 5 Nov — confirm on the portal. The document list below may be shorter than the portal's.

Amount / benefit: Up to ₹50,000 a year (degree); more for medical

Source: Education Department, Govt of Gujarat

What you get

Tuition-fee assistance of up to ₹50,000 a year for degree courses (50% of tuition, whichever is lower) and up to ₹2 lakh for medical, plus hostel and book support in some cases.

Who is eligible

  • Linked to Gujarat (home state or institute state)
  • Family income up to ₹6 lakh per year

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Marksheet showing 80 percentile (Class 10 or 12)
  • Income certificate (Mamlatdar / Talati / TDO)
  • Admission proof
  • Aadhaar card
  • Bank passbook

How to apply, step by step

  1. Open mysy.gujarat.gov.in and register as a Fresh applicant.
  2. Upload your income certificate (issued by the Mamlatdar, Talati or TDO), the marksheet showing your percentile, admission proof and bank details.
  3. Submit the form before the last date and take a printout.
  4. Get your documents verified at your help centre before the verification date shown on the portal.
  5. Track the status on the portal. The support is paid as tuition-fee assistance.
हिंदी में आवेदन कैसे करें
  1. mysy.gujarat.gov.in खोलें और Fresh आवेदक के रूप में रजिस्टर करें।
  2. आय प्रमाण पत्र (मामलातदार, तलाटी या TDO द्वारा जारी), परसेंटाइल दिखाने वाली मार्कशीट, एडमिशन प्रमाण और बैंक की जानकारी अपलोड करें।
  3. अंतिम तारीख से पहले फॉर्म सबमिट करें और उसका प्रिंट निकाल लें।
  4. पोर्टल पर दिखाई गई सत्यापन तारीख से पहले अपने हेल्प सेंटर पर दस्तावेज़ों का सत्यापन करवाएं।
  5. पोर्टल पर स्थिति देखते रहें। सहायता ट्यूशन फीस सहायता के रूप में मिलती है।
