Top Class Education Scheme for SC Students

Ministry of Social Justice scheme for SC students admitted to a full-time course beyond Class 12 in a notified (top-class) institution. Family income up to ₹8 lakh from all sources; no more than 2 siblings per family. Fresh awards go only to first-year students, ranked by merit within the slots given to your institute; the award continues until the course ends. Students who did Class 10 in a government or aided school (except KVS) qualify automatically. NSP 2026-27: portal open from 1 Jun 2026, last date 31 Oct 2026, institute verification 15 Nov, ministry verification 30 Nov.

Amount / benefit: Full tuition (to ₹2 lakh) + allowance

Source: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment

What you get

Full tuition fee and non-refundable charges (up to ₹2 lakh a year in private institutions), plus an academic allowance (₹86,000 in the first year per the NSP FAQ; lower in later years). Confirm current amounts on NSP.

Who is eligible

  • Open to students from any state in India
  • Category: Scheduled Caste (SC)
  • Family income up to ₹8 lakh per year

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Valid SC certificate
  • Income certificate (family income up to ₹8 lakh)
  • Admission proof in a notified institution
  • Fee structure / fee receipt
  • Aadhaar and Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • Class 10 school certificate (for automatic qualification)

How to apply, step by step

  1. Go to scholarships.gov.in and open the Students section. Keep your Aadhaar and mobile number ready.
  2. Do One Time Registration (OTR) first — it uses Aadhaar and face authentication through the NSP OTR app. You get a 14-digit OTR number that works for your whole academic career. Note it down.
  3. Log in with your OTR number and password, then choose 'Apply for Scholarship'.
  4. From the list of schemes, select 'Top Class Education Scheme for SC Students'. Choose the scheme that matches your category.
  5. Fill in personal, course, institute and bank details. The bank account must be your own and Aadhaar-linked.
  6. Upload scanned copies of the documents listed on this card.
  7. Submit the form and save your Application ID. Your application then goes to your school/college for verification.
  8. Track the status by logging in on NSP. If it is marked 'defective', correct it with 'Fill/Edit Application Form' before the deadline.
हिंदी में आवेदन कैसे करें
  1. scholarships.gov.in पर जाकर Students सेक्शन खोलें। अपना आधार और मोबाइल नंबर पास रखें।
  2. सबसे पहले One Time Registration (OTR) करें — इसमें आधार और NSP OTR ऐप से चेहरे की पहचान (face authentication) होती है। आपको 14 अंकों का OTR नंबर मिलेगा जो पूरी पढ़ाई में काम आएगा। इसे लिखकर रख लें।
  3. OTR नंबर और पासवर्ड से लॉगिन करें, फिर 'Apply for Scholarship' चुनें।
  4. योजनाओं की सूची में से 'Top Class Education Scheme for SC Students' चुनें। अपनी कैटेगरी वाली योजना ही चुनें।
  5. व्यक्तिगत, कोर्स, संस्थान और बैंक की जानकारी भरें। बैंक खाता आपका अपना और आधार से लिंक होना चाहिए।
  6. इस कार्ड में दी गई दस्तावेज़ों की सूची के अनुसार उनकी स्कैन कॉपी अपलोड करें।
  7. फॉर्म सबमिट करें और Application ID सेव कर लें। इसके बाद आपका आवेदन आपके स्कूल/कॉलेज के पास सत्यापन के लिए जाता है।
  8. NSP पर लॉगिन करके स्थिति (status) देखते रहें। अगर 'defective' दिखे, तो अंतिम तारीख से पहले 'Fill/Edit Application Form' से सुधार करें।
