UP Post-Matric (Dashmottar) Scholarship — General / OBC / Minority

For General, OBC and Minority students of Uttar Pradesh in graduation, post-graduation, diploma and other post-matric courses (other than Class 11-12). Family income up to ₹2 lakh. Fresh applications close 31 Oct 2026; renewal closed 15 Oct 2026. Class 11-12 (Intermediate) fresh applications already closed on 21 Sep 2026. One third-party site lists ₹1 lakh for minority — confirm on the portal. The document list below may be shorter than the portal's.

Amount / benefit: ≈ ₹2,500–13,500 a year (varies)

Source: Social Welfare Department, Govt of Uttar Pradesh

What you get

Scholarship of roughly ₹2,500–₹13,500 a year depending on class and category (per scheme summaries — confirm on the portal).

Who is eligible

  • Resident (domicile) of Uttar Pradesh
  • Category: General, OBC, EWS, Minority community
  • Family income up to ₹2 lakh per year

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Income certificate
  • Caste certificate (OBC) or minority proof where applicable
  • Aadhaar card
  • Previous year marksheet
  • Fee receipt / admission proof
  • Aadhaar-linked bank passbook

How to apply, step by step

  1. Open scholarship.up.gov.in (Social Welfare Department, UP). Choose Student, then 'Dashmottar / Post-Matric (Other than Intermediate)' and click New Registration (fresh).
  2. Register with your name and mobile number and note down your registration number and password.
  3. Log in and fill the application form: personal, category, income, course, institute and bank details. Your bank account must be your own and Aadhaar-linked.
  4. Upload the documents listed on this card and final-submit the form.
  5. Print the final form and submit the hard copy with your documents at your college before the hard-copy date. Check that exact date in the portal notice.
  6. Your college verifies the application first, then the department. Track the status on the portal; if it is sent back, correct it and submit again.
हिंदी में आवेदन कैसे करें
  1. scholarship.up.gov.in (समाज कल्याण विभाग, उत्तर प्रदेश) खोलें। Student चुनें, फिर 'Dashmottar / Post-Matric (Other than Intermediate)' चुनकर New Registration (नया आवेदन) पर क्लिक करें।
  2. अपने नाम और मोबाइल नंबर से रजिस्ट्रेशन करें, और रजिस्ट्रेशन नंबर व पासवर्ड लिखकर रख लें।
  3. लॉगिन करके आवेदन फॉर्म भरें: व्यक्तिगत, श्रेणी, आय, कोर्स, संस्थान और बैंक की जानकारी। बैंक खाता आपका अपना और आधार से लिंक होना चाहिए।
  4. इस कार्ड में दिए गए दस्तावेज़ अपलोड करें और फॉर्म फाइनल सबमिट करें।
  5. फाइनल फॉर्म का प्रिंट निकालकर, दस्तावेज़ों के साथ हार्ड कॉपी हार्ड-कॉपी की अंतिम तारीख से पहले अपने कॉलेज में जमा करें। सटीक तारीख पोर्टल की सूचना में देखें।
  6. आपका कॉलेज पहले और फिर विभाग आवेदन का सत्यापन करता है। पोर्टल पर स्थिति देखते रहें; अगर आवेदन वापस आए तो सुधार करके दोबारा जमा करें।
