Tamil Pudhalvan Scheme (Tamil Nadu) — ₹1,000 a month for boys

For boy students who studied Class 6 to 12 in a Tamil Nadu government or government-aided school and are now in a regular higher-education course. No family-income limit. Students from private schools are not eligible. Apply through your college. No fixed last date was confirmed — ask your college. The document list below may be shorter than the portal's.

Amount / benefit: ₹1,000 a month (₹12,000 a year)

Source: Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Govt of Tamil Nadu

What you get

₹1,000 every month for the length of the course, paid to your bank account by DBT.

Who is eligible

  • Resident (domicile) of Tamil Nadu
  • Open to male students

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • School study certificates (Class 6 to 12, TN government / aided school)
  • College admission / bonafide certificate
  • Aadhaar card
  • Aadhaar-linked bank passbook

How to apply, step by step

  1. This scheme is applied for through your college. Ask your college office or the scheme nodal officer for the current registration process and last date.
  2. Keep ready: school certificates showing Class 6 to 12 in a Tamil Nadu government or government-aided school, your college admission/bonafide certificate, Aadhaar, and an Aadhaar-linked bank account in your own name.
  3. Give the details and documents to your college, or register on the portal they tell you about.
  4. After verification the ₹1,000 a month is sent to your bank account by DBT. If a month is missed, ask your college to check your status.
