INSPIRE Scholarship for Higher Education (SHE) — DST

Department of Science and Technology scheme to keep top students in the natural and basic sciences. For students aged 17-22 who have just passed Class 12 and join B.Sc./B.S./Integrated M.Sc./M.S. in natural or basic sciences. You must be in the top 1% of your Class 12 board, or have a top rank in JEE/NEET (see the DST notice for the exact cut-offs each year). A gap year after Class 12 is not allowed. About 12,000 awards a year, for up to 5 years with yearly renewal on marks. No income limit. The application window is announced by DST each year; no 2026-27 date was confirmed.

Amount / benefit: ₹80,000 a year

Source: Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India

What you get

₹80,000 a year: ₹60,000 scholarship (₹5,000 a month) plus ₹20,000 for a summer research project.

Who is eligible

  • Open to students from any state in India
  • Age 17 to 22 years
  • Education level: Class 12, Bachelor's degree (UG)
  • Science stream

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Class 12 marksheet (proof of top 1% / board percentile)
  • Class 10 marksheet
  • JEE / NEET rank card (if using the rank route)
  • Admission proof in a natural-science degree course
  • Aadhaar and bank account in your name

How to apply, step by step

  1. Go to the INSPIRE portal (online-inspire.gov.in) and open the Scholarship for Higher Education (SHE) section. Applications open once a year, so read the DST notice for the current window first.
  2. Register with your email ID and mobile number and verify both.
  3. Fill in your Class 12 board details (and JEE/NEET rank if you apply through the rank route), then your course and college details.
  4. Upload the scanned documents listed on this card.
  5. Submit the form and note down your application number.
  6. Keep checking the portal and your email for the selection list. The scholarship is renewed every year if you meet the marks condition in the scheme guidelines.
