Rajasthan Post-Matric Scholarship (SJE) — SC / ST

For SC and ST students who are permanent residents of Rajasthan, in Class 11 up to postgraduate and professional courses. Family income up to ₹2.5 lakh. Your institute must be registered on the SJE portal. Amount depends on course level and hostel or home. The document list below may be shorter than the portal's.

Amount / benefit: Maintenance + fees + books (varies by course)

Source: Social Justice and Empowerment Department, Govt of Rajasthan

What you get

Maintenance allowance, compulsory fees, books and study-tour expenses; amount depends on course and hostel/day-scholar status (per scheme summaries — confirm on the portal).

Who is eligible

  • Resident (domicile) of Rajasthan
  • Category: Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST)
  • Family income up to ₹2.5 lakh per year

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Rajasthan domicile proof (Jan Aadhaar / domicile certificate)
  • Caste certificate (SC/ST)
  • Income certificate
  • Previous year marksheet
  • Fee receipt
  • Aadhaar-linked bank passbook

How to apply, step by step

  1. Open the Rajasthan scholarship portal (sje.rajasthan.gov.in) and log in with your Rajasthan SSO ID. If you don't have one, create it at sso.rajasthan.gov.in first.
  2. Open the Post-Matric Scholarship application (fresh or renewal) for the new session.
  3. Fill in personal, category, income, course and bank details, and select your institute. Your institute must be registered on the portal, otherwise it won't appear.
  4. Upload the documents listed on this card and submit the form. Save your application number.
  5. Your institute verifies the application, then the department. Track the status on the portal and fix anything that is sent back before the last date.
हिंदी में आवेदन कैसे करें
  1. राजस्थान स्कॉलरशिप पोर्टल (sje.rajasthan.gov.in) खोलें और अपनी राजस्थान SSO ID से लॉगिन करें। SSO ID न हो तो पहले sso.rajasthan.gov.in पर बनाएं।
  2. नए सत्र के लिए Post-Matric Scholarship का आवेदन (नया या नवीनीकरण) खोलें।
  3. व्यक्तिगत, श्रेणी, आय, कोर्स और बैंक की जानकारी भरें और अपना संस्थान चुनें। संस्थान का पोर्टल पर रजिस्टर होना ज़रूरी है, वरना वह सूची में नहीं दिखेगा।
  4. इस कार्ड में दिए गए दस्तावेज़ अपलोड करें और फॉर्म सबमिट करें। अपना आवेदन नंबर सेव कर लें।
  5. आपका संस्थान पहले और फिर विभाग आवेदन का सत्यापन करता है। पोर्टल पर स्थिति देखें और अंतिम तारीख से पहले वापस आई कमियाँ ठीक करें।
