Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana — Graduation Incentive (Bihar)

Bihar Education Department incentive for an unmarried girl who has passed her graduation from a recognised university or college in Bihar. You must be a Bihar domicile. Apply online on the Medhasoft portal when the window opens, and your college must have sent your result to the portal. NOTE: sources disagree on the amount (most say ₹50,000; one says ₹25,000), on the portal address (medhasoft.bihar.gov.in or medhasoft.bih.nic.in) and on helpline numbers. Confirm everything on the official portal before you press Verify. The 31 Oct 2026 date quoted by one site is not confirmed, so no deadline is set.

Amount / benefit: ₹50,000 one-time (confirm)

Source: Education Department, Government of Bihar

What you get

One-time cash incentive paid by DBT to your bank account (reported ₹50,000; confirm the current amount).

Who is eligible

  • Resident (domicile) of Bihar
  • Open to female students
  • Education level: Bachelor's degree (UG)

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • Graduation marksheet / degree certificate
  • Bihar domicile (residence) certificate
  • Declaration that you are unmarried
  • Aadhaar and bank account in your name

How to apply, step by step

  1. Check that your college has sent your graduation result to the Bihar Medhasoft portal.
  2. Open the Medhasoft portal of the Bihar Education Department (medhasoft.bihar.gov.in) when the Kanya Utthan window is open.
  3. Choose the Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana graduation component and register or log in.
  4. Fill in your details and upload the documents listed on this card, including the unmarried declaration.
  5. Submit and note the application number. After verification the money is paid by DBT, so keep your bank account active and Aadhaar-linked.
हिंदी में आवेदन कैसे करें
  1. पक्का करें कि आपके कॉलेज ने आपका स्नातक का रिज़ल्ट बिहार के मेधासॉफ्ट पोर्टल पर भेज दिया है।
  2. कन्या उत्थान की विंडो खुली हो तब बिहार शिक्षा विभाग का मेधासॉफ्ट पोर्टल (medhasoft.bihar.gov.in) खोलें।
  3. मुख्यमंत्री कन्या उत्थान योजना का स्नातक वाला हिस्सा चुनें और रजिस्टर या लॉगिन करें।
  4. अपनी जानकारी भरें और इस कार्ड पर बताए दस्तावेज़ (अविवाहित होने की घोषणा सहित) अपलोड करें।
  5. सबमिट करें और आवेदन संख्या लिख लें। जाँच के बाद पैसा DBT से आता है, इसलिए बैंक खाता चालू और आधार से लिंक रखें।
