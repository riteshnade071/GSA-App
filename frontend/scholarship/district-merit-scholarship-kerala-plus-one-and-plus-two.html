District Merit Scholarship (Kerala, Plus One and Plus Two)

Merit award of the Directorate of Collegiate Education, Kerala, for students who got A+ in all subjects in SSLC and now study in a government or aided higher-secondary school in Kerala (Plus One and Plus Two). No family income limit. Apply online on the DCE scholarship portal. The yearly last date is announced by DCE; secondary sources mention 30 Nov 2026, which is NOT confirmed. Helpline 9446096580.

Amount / benefit: ₹1,250 a year

Source: Directorate of Collegiate Education (DCE), Government of Kerala

What you get

₹1,250 a year during Plus One and Plus Two.

Who is eligible

  • Resident (domicile) of Kerala
  • Education level: Class 12

Final eligibility is decided by the official authority. Read the official notification before you apply.

Documents required

  • SSLC marksheet showing A+ in all subjects
  • Kerala native / domicile proof
  • Higher-secondary school enrolment details
  • Bank account details

How to apply, step by step

  1. Open the DCE Kerala scholarship portal (dcescholarship.kerala.gov.in) and register as a student.
  2. Choose the scholarship (District Merit Scholarship) from the list.
  3. Enter your SSLC details and upload the marksheet that shows A+ in all subjects.
  4. Enter your higher-secondary school and enrolment details, then check everything.
  5. Submit the form, note the application number, and check the portal for the result. For help call 9446096580.
