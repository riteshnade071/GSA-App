About OpportunityHub

OpportunityHub is a free, independent website that helps students and farmers in India find government scholarships and schemes in one place, and check which ones may suit them.

What we do

We collect scheme details from official government sources, write them in simple language, and link to the official website for every scheme so you can apply there. We do not charge any fee and we do not apply on your behalf.

How we keep information correct

Every scheme page shows its official source. Deadlines and amounts can change, so please confirm on the official website before applying. If you find a mistake, email us and we will fix it.

Independent project

We are not a government body and not affiliated with any scheme listed here. Read our Terms and Privacy Policy.
